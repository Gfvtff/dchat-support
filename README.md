## Troubleshooting / 常见问题

- Nearby discovery: both people must enable Bluetooth and keep DChat open nearby. Discovery does not automatically trust or add a contact; confirm the person and verify the fingerprint.
- Remote chat: both devices need internet connectivity and compatible Relay settings. “Submitted” does not mean “delivered”; delivery requires an encrypted acknowledgement from the receiving app.
- Identity: no phone number or email registration is required. Uninstalling DChat does not provide a way to recover the old identity or local chat history. Do not reinstall as a troubleshooting step if you need those records.
- Deletion: deleting a conversation does not erase screenshots, copied content, or external copies. A request to delete on both devices depends on the other client receiving and processing it.
- Screen privacy: iOS reports screenshots after they occur. DChat cannot guarantee prevention of every screenshot or photography by another device.

## Safety concerns / 骚扰与安全问题

Decline unexpected contact requests. You can remove a contact from the app. Contact removal is not a promise to block every future invitation from that person. To report a safety concern, contact the support email above and share only the information you choose to disclose. Do not publish sensitive reports as public GitHub issues.

请拒绝不认识的联系申请。可在 App 中删除联系人，但删除不代表永久阻止该身份再次邀请。如需反映骚扰或安全问题，请通过上方邮箱联系，只提供你自愿披露的信息，勿在公开 Issue 中发布敏感内容。
